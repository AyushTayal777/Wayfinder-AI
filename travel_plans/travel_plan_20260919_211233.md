### Booking Hacks for September

5. **Flex‑ticket:** September can still be rainy; a refundable or change‑allowed ticket (≈ ₹ 1 200 extra) saves headaches if you need to shift a day.

**Why these zones?**  
- **North Goa (Candolim/Calangute/Anjuna)** – best for nightlife, beach parties, water‑sport rentals (when the sea calms).

- **South Goa (Palolem/Agonda)** – quieter, ideal for sunset walks and relaxed meals; you can stay a night or two here (see Day 5 itinerary).

*Tip:* Book a **mix‑and‑match** stay – 3 nights in North Goa (mid‑range), 2 nights in South Goa (budget), 2 nights back in Panaji (luxury or mid‑range) to experience all vibes without excessive travel.
